Resume folders
==============

Current/
  resume.pdf   ← Active file the site serves (/resume/Current/resume.pdf).

Archive/
  …            ← Old PDFs when you rotate versions (any filename you like).

When you update
---------------
1. Move Current/resume.pdf → Archive/<e.g. resume-2025-03.pdf>
2. Save the new PDF as Current/resume.pdf

Config: src/lib/site.ts → RESUME_PATH
